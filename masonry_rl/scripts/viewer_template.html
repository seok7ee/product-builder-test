Unofficial approximation generated humanoid · masonry RL bring-up

Not a Boston Dynamics asset. No public URDF or USD exists for the electric Atlas. This model is generated from published figures — 1.9 m, 90 kg, 56 DoF, 2.3 m span — with segment lengths and masses from Winter's anthropometric tables. Link shapes are primitives; inertias and joint limits are estimates. Good for workspace, reach and balance bring-up. Not a source of Atlas results.
